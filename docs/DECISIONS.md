# Decisions

## Phase 0 review (2026-11-12): stay code-built, raise the quality

**Decision (owner):** keep code-built visuals for everything, characters included. No paid AI asset
tools (stage 2 is not needed).

**Direction:** keep the current style's handcrafted, slightly imperfect charm, but make it read as
premium: sharper, cleaner, more detailed, more refined. Not AAA realism; a polished, deliberate
stylized look.

**What that means in practice**
- Sharpness first: crisper image (anti-aliasing that doesn't blur, careful resolution budgeting),
  cleaner edges and silhouettes, fine detail that survives on a phone screen.
- Characters, code-built: better proportions and silhouettes, smoother sculpted forms, layered hair
  with proper highlights, expressive faces with expressions, richer clothing, more fluid motion.
- World: more detail where the player looks (ground close-up, foliage, props), richer lighting and
  contact shading, without losing phone performance.
- UI: the ornate pass (#39).
- Every visual issue is checked at real phone pixel density before sign-off.
